The Vision

Business runs in our veins

Inspired by this guidance, and with the Dua Mubarak of Aqa Moula (TUS), Anjuman-e-Saifee Chicago and Al-Tijaarat al-Raabehah Chicago present a premier two-day Business & Innovation Showcase.

The Biz Connect Showcase brings together entrepreneurs, business owners, professionals, manufacturers, retailers, innovators, startups, investors and aspiring leaders — to connect, collaborate and grow.

More than an exhibition, it is a platform to exchange ideas, discover opportunities, build lasting partnerships and strengthen the economic future of our community.

Two days of keynotes, working sessions, a full exhibition floor and deliberate networking, hosted at the spectacular Compass Arena in Willowbrook, Illinois. A state-of-the-art venue, and the right setting for business that starts as conversation and ends as partnership.

Registration is open. Complete your registration today and be part of a gathering where businesses connect, collaborate and thrive.

Why take part

Great businesses are not built alone

They are built through vision, collaboration and meaningful relationships. Two days in one room is enough time to start all three.

For Attendees

Why attend

  • Hear from leaders who have built businesses within and beyond the community.
  • Find the trends, tools and technology reshaping your sector before your competition does.
  • Build relationships that outlast the weekend — suppliers, partners, mentors, customers.
  • Walk the exhibition floor and see what Mumineen across North America are making and selling.
  • Bring a specific problem; leave with people who have already solved it.
  • Open doors for the next generation entering business.

For Exhibitors

Why exhibit

  • Put your product in front of decision-makers who buy, distribute and refer.
  • Generate qualified leads across two days of concentrated footfall.
  • Launch or demonstrate something new to an audience primed to look.
  • Meet distributors, retailers and manufacturers you would otherwise cold-email.
  • Build brand recognition across the North American Bohra business community.
  • Sit alongside businesses at every stage — and learn from the floor as much as you sell on it.

Questions

Before you register

Who is this showcase for?

Anyone doing business or preparing to. Established owners, professionals, manufacturers and retailers will find buyers and partners; startups, students and first-time founders will find mentors and a first audience. You do not need a booth to get value from both days.

How do I register?

Registration runs through a single form covering both attendees and exhibitors — you choose which on the last question. Have your ITS number and Jamaat to hand, along with your industry and business name. The committee will follow up with the details for your pass, including payment once rates are confirmed.

Can I attend for only one day?

The programme is designed to build across both days, and passes are being planned around full attendance. If a single day is your only option, note it on the registration form and the committee will come back to you.

What does an exhibitor booth include?

Booth space in the Business Expo & Product Showcase, a directory listing, and attendee access for your booth team. Booth sizes, layouts and rates are being finalised — register and select Exhibitor to be first in line.

Is food available at the venue?

Yes. Food stalls and vendors will be on site across both days serving halal food. Compass Arena is a combined arena and dining venue. There are also halal restaurants a few minutes away if you would rather go out — several are listed above.

I want to sponsor or speak. Who do I contact?

Reach out through the contact details below and the organising committee will send the sponsorship pack and speaking guidelines.
